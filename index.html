Bulk QR Labels

Generate QR codes for an entire CSV, with filenames from your SKU or URL column, a print-ready label PDF, and an automatic decode-check that every code actually scans.

Free100% in your browserNo sign-up

Runs 100% in your browser. Your files never leave this device.

How it works

  • CSV, XLSX or a pasted list, with encode-column and filename-column mapping.
  • URL templates with {column} placeholders build each code from a row.
  • Pick an error-correction level, with a safe-size warning for logo overlays.
  • Download a PNG and SVG ZIP with manifest.csv, or an Avery label-sheet PDF.
  • Decode-verify a sample or every code with jsQR and get a failure report.
